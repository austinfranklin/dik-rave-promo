Getting here

Svarta Lådan, room MB313
Moas båge, Södertörn University
Alfred Nobels allé 7, Flemingsberg
141 89 Huddinge

Take the commuter train (pendeltåg) to Flemingsberg. From the platform, take the escalators up to the campus. Moas båge, the university’s main building, is across the square from the library, a few minutes’ walk from the station.

Buses stop at Södertörns högskola and Blickagången.
